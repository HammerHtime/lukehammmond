About Luke

[ action photo of Luke — click to upload ]
Change photo

Luke Hammond is a 14-year-old competitive swimmer from Lakeshore Swim Club in Etobicoke, Ontario who started racing in the spring of 2022. An all-round swimmer, Luke excels across freestyle, backstroke, and IM — currently ranked top 5 in Canada in the 200m, 400m, and 800m freestyle, and top 15 in the 200m backstroke and 400m IM for his age group.

He trains 6 days a week and spends over 15 hours in the pool every single week. Early mornings, late nights.

This past year he was named to the Swim Canada National ID Development Program and the Swim Ontario Aerobic Development Program — pretty cool recognition that he's heading in the right direction.

Get In Touch

The Path to 2029

I'm a distance freestyle specialist, and right now, I'm ranked in the top 5 in Canada across three of my main events. I actually love the grind of the 800m and 1500m—I'm the kind of swimmer who stays focused on the technical details and splits when the sets get long.

Beyond the times, the team environment is a huge part of why I show up every day. I get along with everyone and love the energy of a fast lane where we're all pushing each other to be better. I'm highly coachable, I take feedback seriously, and I'm always looking for ways to improve.

My goal is to bring that work ethic and team-first attitude to a NCAA Division I program. I'm excited to keep climbing the rankings and would welcome the chance to talk with coaches and scouts about how I can contribute to their team.
