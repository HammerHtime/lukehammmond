Get In Touch

The Path to 2029

I'm a distance freestyle specialist, and right now, I'm ranked in the top 5 in Canada across three of my main events. I actually love the grind of the 800m and 1500m—I'm the kind of swimmer who stays focused on the technical details and splits when the sets get long.

Beyond the times, the team environment is a huge part of why I show up every day. I get along with everyone and love the energy of a fast lane where we're all pushing each other to be better. I'm highly coachable, I take feedback seriously, and I'm always looking for ways to improve.

My goal is to bring that work ethic and team-first attitude to a university programme, in Canada or the United States. I'm excited to keep climbing the rankings and would welcome the chance to talk with coaches about how I can contribute to their team.
