# What college coaches want on a recruit's page

Research done 1 October 2026 by three research agents, then checked against the
site. Anything a source could not confirm is marked unverified. Personal details
were kept out of the searches.

## The short version

Both countries' coaches want the same core: best times with course, meet and
date, graduation year, intended study, academics, club and club coach, and a way
to reach the swimmer. The differences that change the page:

- **US coaches race yards.** Show yards conversions, clearly labelled as
  estimates, beside the real metre times. Long course still counts: the UVA head
  coach said "Majorly!" when asked (Swimming World, June 2026,
  swimmingworldmagazine.com/news/does-long-course-matter-swimming-recruiting/).
- **Canadian coaches race short course metres.** U SPORTS is a short course
  championship. The page showed long course only until 1 October 2026.
- **Academics.** US forms ask for GPA. Canadian forms ask for marks, and Ontario
  uses a percentage average. Show the real percentage, not only a converted GPA.
- **US D1 contact opens 15 June 2027 for class of 2029.** U SPORTS schools and
  recruits may contact each other at any time (Policy 40.10.7, unverified that
  the 2019 text is current).

## What the questionnaires ask for

US, 5 forms (UVA D1, Tampa D2, Nova Southeastern D2, Colorado College D3,
Indiana Wesleyan NAIA): best times, GPA, intended major, club and coach contact,
height and grad year on all five. Video on three. NCAA ID on two (the D1 and D2
forms). None ask for splits, training volume or a meet schedule.

Canada, 8 forms (Toronto, UBC, Calgary, Western, Laurier, McMaster, Victoria,
McGill): best times, events, grad year, intended program, club and club coach on
all eight. Marks and high school on seven. Height on seven. Video on one (UBC).
Only Toronto's is swim specific; the rest are generic all-sport forms.

Height, date of birth, address and parent contacts belong on those private forms,
not on a public page for a minor.

## Rules worth knowing

- **U SPORTS athletic awards:** the 80% entering-average rule was dropped from
  2024-25. Admission is now the gate (godinos.com, 28 September 2023). Many guides
  still quote 80%. Awards cover tuition and fees at most. OUA cap $5,000
  (oua.ca, unverified for 2026-27).
- **U SPORTS Letter of Intent:** signed only in the 12 months before the academic
  year, ie, grade 12, and it ends recruiting by every other school.
- **U SPORTS:** five years of eligibility, not four.
- **NCAA:** SAT and ACT are not required for D1 or D2 eligibility. D3 treats a
  Canadian as international and needs an Eligibility Center account. A free
  profile account can be opened now. D1 needs 16 core courses; how Canadian
  courses map is set by NCAA provincial lists.
- **Visa:** Canadians need no visa stamp but do need an I-20, the SEVIS fee and
  proof of funds.

## What coaches dislike

A parent running the contact ("a HUGE red flag", Coach Kristy, 2021). Times not
labelled by course or not converted. Stale times. Copy-paste emails. Padding the
list with weak events. Information behind a login.

## No real personal sites found

The first agent could not find a single real swimmer's personal recruiting site
it could open and check. Search engines do not index them and direct searches
were blocked. It did not invent any. Its counts are from ten published guides.
